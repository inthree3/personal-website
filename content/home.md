# Hi, I’m Stella.<br>A full-stack engineer building practical ML and agentic systems.

I'm a research-minded engineer working on [MySky](https://bsky.app/profile/greenearth.social) at UC Berkeley's [Center for Human-Compatible AI (CHAI)](https://humancompatible.ai/), building an autonomous operations system and a recommender for constructive conversations. I'm fortunate to be supervised by [Jonathan Stray](https://jonathanstray.com/).

Before studying at UC Berkeley, I've worked as a software engineer at [National Assembly (South Korea)](https://assembly.go.kr/portal/main/main.do) with [Lee Jun-seok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Jun-seok). There, we launched an initiative to explore how AI agents could support political workflows at scale.

I’ve developed two early-stage ventures, succeed on raising fund, and also worked in a venture capital with a focus on early-stage startups.

## Currently thinking about

-   **Distributed systems** How can distributed systems coordinate work and stay reliable as they grow?
-   **Agent swarms** How can agent swarms share context and coordinate toward a common goal?
-   **Recommendation systems** How can recommendation systems learn from feedback and adapt to changing interests?
