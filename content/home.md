# Hi, I’m Stella, a full-stack engineer focused on AI and data.

My background spans entrepreneurship, research, and software development. I’ve developed early-stage ventures, including MEFETO and FionaHan.

However, we couldn’t take either venture further because we struggled with data acquisition.

I want to build reliable, observable environments where people can try new ideas with confidence. I’m interested in distributed systems, agent swarms, and recommendation systems. Across these areas, I’m curious about how components coordinate, learn from feedback, and work reliably at scale.

## Currently thinking about

- **Distributed systems** How can distributed systems coordinate work and stay reliable as they grow?
- **Agent swarms** How can agent swarms share context and coordinate toward a common goal?
- **Recommendation systems** How can recommendation systems learn from feedback and adapt to changing interests?
